Rotating magnetic field

Two windings, 90° apart, fed by currents 90° out of phase. Watch the resultant field rotate.

Phase 1 = i₁ = cos θ (left–right poles), phase 2 = i₂ = sin θ (top–bottom poles). The green arrow is the sum of the two fluxes; its tip is the “North pole” of the stator field, as in the video. Tap the waveform or a point number to jump to a time.
