R-2R Ladder DAC

Flip a bit switch, type a code, or tap a step below. The ladder solves itself and Vout updates live.

Output

1011
0 V
0 V

Each bit adds its own share

Superposition: with only bit k on, the ladder gives Vref ÷ 2n. The total is the sum of the shares.

All 16 output steps (tap one)

Ladder values: R = 10 kΩ, 2R = 20 kΩ, output unloaded. A bit at 1 connects its 2R leg to Vref, a bit at 0 connects it to 0 V. Current arrows show real flow: ↑ into the ladder, ↓ out to 0 V.
