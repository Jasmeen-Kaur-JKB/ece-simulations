3-Phase Rotating Magnetic Field

Three coils, three alternating currents shifted by 120°. Together they make one magnetic field that spins.

Stator (top view)

Currents in the three coils

1. Three coils

Coils A, B and C sit 120° apart around the ring. Each one makes a magnetic field along its own axis when current flows.

2. Shifted currents

Each current rises and falls like a wave, but the waves peak one after another, 120° apart. When one coil is at its peak, the others are partly on.

3. Fields add up

The three coil fields add as arrows. The black arrow is their sum. Its strength stays the same, but its direction keeps turning.

4. Reverse it

Swap any two phases and the sum arrow spins the other way. This is how a 3-phase motor is reversed.
